# JLCMC BoM

Prices are JLCMC unit prices in USD, taken from the jlcmc.com price API on 2026-11-09, at the quantity tier for the quantity shown.
Prices exclude shipping, EU VAT (22%) and wood panels.
Profile finish: H6 = natural (silver) sandblasted anodized. B2 = black, W1 = white, for about +5–7%.

## Printer workstation: two towers (930 W x 420 D x 1150 H frame, +24 mm end cap +71 mm caster = 1245 mm)

Layout: two identical bays, each 420 mm wide inside.
- Each bay has a tower above the deck, sized for a Bambu P1S with an AMS on top.
- Below the deck, each bay has a bottom shelf and a middle shelf.

Generated by `frame_bom.py`. The order file is `jlcmc_bom.xlsx` (JLCMC "BOM / RFQ" template); the same content is in `jlcmc_bom.csv`.
Quantities are order quantities: small parts include about 10% spare, and the Remark column shows how many are needed.
The configurator in `web/index.html` builds the same BoM for other sizes.

How it fastens together (no drilling or tapping of the aluminium):
- Each TPEA bracket uses 2 x (M6x12 + washer + drop-in T-nut).
- Each TPRE end cap clamps its post with 2 x (M6x12 + T-nut).
- Each panel uses 3 x M6x20 countersunk screws (flush) into T-nuts.

- Layer CUT holds the outline and the Ø6.5 through holes. Layer CSK90_D12.5_TOP marks the 90° countersinks to Ø12.5, cut from the top face.
- Notches are plain rectangles with 1 mm clearance around the posts. Inside corners are R3 (6 mm end mill), outside corners R5.
- The bottom beams sit 60 mm above the post ends so their brackets clear the TPRE clamps.

Clear space:
- Tower: 508 mm above the deck panel, so a P1S (458 mm) has 50 mm of head room.
- Bottom and middle compartments: 213 mm each (brackets take about 35 mm within 35 mm of each post).
- Deck surface 707 mm above the floor, tower top 1257 mm, about 1483 mm with an AMS 2 Pro on top.

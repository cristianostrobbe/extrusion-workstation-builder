    JLCMC order

    Columns match JLCMC's BOM template. Upload the .xlsx under BOM / RFQ on jlcmc.com, or paste the part numbers into the cart one by one. Click a part number to copy it.

    CAD files

    The STEP model is an assembly of the 3030 profiles, floor panels and acrylic, in mm, with the post bottoms at caster height. Brackets and screws aren't modelled. The panel drawings are 1:1 DXF files, one per unique shape, sorted into doors, side panels and floor. Both come as .zip files.
